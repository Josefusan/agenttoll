Business model PLAN

PLAN. Open-source core stays free. No take rate in the gateway.
  • Hosted AgentToll: we run gateway and dashboard; flat monthly tier plus a fee on settled volume, billed off-chain. pay_to stays the founder's.
  • Payout destinations: the founder's own off-ramp account as pay_to, with a threshold sweeper.
  • Agency bundle: AgentToll on every Website Factory site, dashboard per client, line item on the retainer.
  • Pro dashboard: multi-site, exports, alerts, the unbilled-traffic report.

What the accelerator money builds

  • Hosted AgentToll: multi-tenant gateway, Postgres ledger, one dashboard per site.
  • Batch settlement: x402 payment channels on Solana mainnet for sub-cent, high-frequency traffic.
  • Off-ramp partners: payout destinations created in the founder's own account, non-custodial, operated by Clark Technology Ventures.
  • More chains: the gateway has no chain-specific money logic, the facilitator does the chain work.
